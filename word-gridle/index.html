What is Word Gridle?

Word Gridle is a deduction game for people who like words. Rather than hunting through a jumble of letters, you start with blank rows, one per hidden word, so all you know at first is how long each answer is.

The letters on offer are exactly the ones those answers need, which already narrows things down. From there it's a mix of vocabulary and reasoning: try a word that could fit, see which letters it fixes in place, and use that to crack the rows around it. Near misses help as much as hits, and a row can finish itself from letters you placed while chasing a different word.

  1. Read the shapeBlank rows tell you how many words there are and how long.

  2. Try a wordAny letter that lands in the right spot lights up, hit or miss.

  3. Open the chestFill every row and the treasure is yours.

Why it's different

Thinking, not scanning

There's no maze of letters to search. You reason from what the board shows you, so a sharp guess beats a lucky one.

Mistakes still teach you

A guess that misses still shows you something, so you keep moving forward instead of starting over.

Everyday words

The answers are common words you already know, which keeps it fair, and spotting them quickly is good vocabulary practice.
