**KITE GNU/Linux 20.04** continued Kerala's open-source education initiative, part of the same project that began as IT@School GNU/Linux in 2006. For the full background on the project's history and Kerala's open-source education curriculum, see the [KITE GNU/Linux 22.04](/kite-linux-2204-and-opensource-education/) post.
